Pull List

Your open pull requests and issues, in one calm list.

Pull List shows the pull requests you've written or been assigned, plus your assigned issues, with the state that matters at a glance: required checks, review status, Copilot code review, and merge queue. Tag each one with an emoji and a priority, put them in your own order, and see which ones are waiting on you.

Features

  • Required checks only, so an optional job failing doesn't turn a pull request red
  • Review, Copilot code review, and merge queue status side by side
  • A "ready to ship" badge when a pull request is approved, green, and clear of open findings
  • Your own emoji, priority, and manual ordering
  • A subtle highlight on rows where you're the next person who needs to act
  • One collapsed "next step" pill when you want less on screen
  • Redaction for screenshots

Requirements

  • macOS 14 or later
  • A GitHub account

Privacy

Pull List doesn't collect any data. There are no Pull List accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking, and no servers of ours.

  • GitHub access: You sign in with GitHub. Pull List uses that access only to read your pull requests, issues, and their status, and to take actions you choose, like requesting a review. Your sign-in is stored in your device's Keychain.
  • Your tags: Emoji, priorities, and ordering are stored only on your device.
  • GitHub: Requests go directly from your device to GitHub, and are covered by GitHub's privacy statement.

To remove everything, sign out and delete the app. You can also revoke Pull List's access anytime in your GitHub settings under Applications.
